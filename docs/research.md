## Customer test — top 5

### 1. Failed-payment / involuntary-churn recovery on Stripe — PASS
1. **Who pays:** the founder of a $1k–$50k MRR SaaS billed through Stripe, no billing engineer.
2. **Last week:** 3 renewals failed; Stripe retried, sent its generic email, then canceled 2 of the subscriptions. The founder found out from a dip in the MRR chart, if at all.
3. **Today:** nothing ("hand-roll your own retry logic on a Saturday"), or a monthly diff of dashboard MRR vs. settled balance.
4. **Ignore it:** 2–9% of MRR/month leaks; "Almost 30% of my churn wasn't even a decision. Failed payments. Card expired." (IH)
5. **ChatGPT:** can write one email; cannot watch Stripe, know who failed, send on day 0/3/7/14, stop when paid, or count what came back.
6. **Why $15 every month:** new failures every month; the dashboard shows recovered $ next to the $15 fee. Anyone above ~$2k MRR recovers more than the fee in month one.
7. **Trigger:** noticing `past_due`/`unpaid` in Stripe, an IH thread on involuntary churn, searching "stripe failed payment email not working".
8. **60 seconds:** paste a restricted key → "$1,240 at risk across 7 customers; 4 subscriptions already canceled by failed cards; nobody has contacted them."
9. **Reach without ads:** r/SaaS, r/indiehackers, Indie Hackers failed-payment threads (very active), SEO on "stripe smart retries exhausted".
10. **Cancellation:** MRR too small to have failures; Stripe ships founder-voice win-back natively; a month with $0 recovered.

### 2. Contractor estimate follow-up — FAIL on build/distribution
Pain and value are excellent ("I feel like I lose the job half the time"; Jobber $39–49 is the only option with follow-ups). Fails on: SMS is the required channel and US A2P 10DLC registration takes days–weeks before a single text can be sent; r/Contractor mods report "five of these posts every day"; the aha moment is delayed (value shows up days later, not in 60 seconds).

### 3. Freelancer invoice chasing — FAIL on competition
Wave ($19), Invoice Ninja ($14), FreshBooks ($23), Bonsai, HoneyBook and RemindFox ($19) all already do this. An IH validation thread on exactly this concluded "a feature, not a product."

### 4. Silent proposal follow-up — FAIL on integration
Detecting sent quotes and sending from the user's address requires Gmail restricted scopes (Google verification + CASA assessment); the manual-entry version has no instant magic moment.

### 5. Stripe dispute evidence packet — FAIL on frequency
Real pain and fee hatred (Chargeflow's 25%), but an indie SaaS sees 0–3 disputes a month; months with zero disputes make a subscription feel pointless. Kept as a future add-on to #1 (same Stripe connection).

## Winner

**For an indie SaaS founder billing through Stripe, it automatically finds every customer whose card failed — including the ones Stripe already gave up on and canceled — and runs a founder-voice win-back sequence with a one-click pay link until the money comes back, so they no longer have to notice the MRR dip weeks later and write awkward emails by hand.**

Positioning: *Stripe retries the card. Nobody retries the customer.*

Why this over the runners-up: instant, dollar-denominated aha moment; a single self-serve integration (a Stripe restricted key, no OAuth review); the builder's own community is the customer; recurring trigger (new failures every month); recovered $ on the dashboard justifies the fee every single month.

## Pre-mortem

| Kill attempt | Verdict | Fix baked into the concept |
|---|---|---|
| Stripe already does this (Smart Retries, failed-payment emails, expiring-card emails) | Partly true — during the retry window | Stripe stops when retries are exhausted: the subscription becomes canceled/unpaid and no further emails go out. Product leads with the "already lost" bucket and detects it via `cancellation_details.reason = payment_failed`. During the retry window it layers a personal founder-voice email over Stripe's generic one. |
| The $19–29 tier is filling up (Rebounce, ChurnWard, WorkAid, Rebill, MRRSaver) | True | Differentiate on win-back after cancellation (most tools only cover `past_due`), founder-voice drafts, LTV-prioritised "call this one" flags, and a free tier that shows the loss before asking for money. Speed matters; no moat beyond execution is claimed. |
| Nobody pays because their MRR is too small | True below ~$1.5k MRR | Free tier: unlimited scans + 3 sequences/month. Paid tier is only worth it once failures exceed that, which is exactly when it pays for itself. |
| Emails from a third-party domain look spammy | Risk | Send as `Founder Name <recover@mail.domain>` with Reply-To the founder; Pro tier adds a verified custom sending domain. Every email links to Stripe's own hosted invoice / checkout page, so customers never enter a card on our site. |
| Founders won't paste an API key | Friction | Restricted key with exactly four read permissions + Checkout write; the UI shows the exact Stripe screen and validates scopes on paste. Stripe Connect OAuth is the v2 path. |
| A month with zero failures → churn | Real | Dashboard keeps "cards expiring in the next 30 days" and cumulative recovered $ visible; pre-expiry nudges give the product something to do every month. |
| Stripe adds post-cancellation win-back natively | Possible | Accept the risk; it is the same risk every dunning company carries and the segment has paid $49–$250/mo for years anyway. |

The concept survives: the objections change the positioning (win-back after exhaustion, not "another dunning tool") but do not invalidate the pain, the frequency, or the economics.
